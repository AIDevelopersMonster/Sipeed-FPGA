## Architectural boundary
The Sipeed video example is an independent FPGA configuration and is **not** a LiteX + HDMI integrated SoC. Passing this example shows the board/HDMI path can produce test video and respond to a key in the temporary image. It does **not** establish video CSR, a CPU-driven framebuffer, arbitration to SDRAM, or simultaneous LiteX BIOS/HDMI operation.
