## Remaining critical path (lean-UART candidate)

From `mem_ctrl/ck_e_p_s0/Q` on `pll/rpll_inst/CLKOUT.default_gen_clk:[R]` to `mem_ctrl/oddr_ck/D0` on `pll/rpll_inst/CLKOUTP.default_gen_clk:[R]`.

- Period of each generated clock: 12.346 ns (81 MHz).
- Phase difference: 3.086 ns (nominal 90 degrees).
- **Setup slack: -0.570 ns**, reported as 1 Setup-violated endpoint of 480.
- Main-domain Fmax of 86.793 MHz does **not** authorize this separately constrained phase-crossing path.

## Remaining evidence gaps / synthesis cautions

- Phase-shifted clock `CLKOUTP` has no reported frequency path in the summary. The DDR clock interface is not fully timing-qualified.
- The simple SDC constrains the 27MHz input. Full PSRAM DDR interface input/output timing, phase, and coverage require explicit independent review.
- Gowin warned that `TX` inputs of two DDR primitives were undriven and assumed zero. This was **not** dispositioned as safe; other `Q1` unconnected warnings also remain.
- Second-die signals `O_psram_ck[1]`, `O_psram_cs_n[1]`, `IO_psram_rwds[1]` and `IO_psram_dq[15:8]` were unused or not driven. The reference intentionally addresses only the first die, so these warnings are **not themselves proof of a defect**, but full 8MiB functionality cannot be claimed.
- The conventional PnR user-pin table lists clock/reset/UART/LED pins, not special PSRAM physical ports. Thus this listing by itself does not qualify the internal memory attachment.
- Recovery/Removal reported "Nothing to report!", which does **not** establish reset release timing correctness.
- There was no PSRAM electrical model verification and **zero** LAB-07 hardware attempts. The planned 1MiB address-pattern read/write did not run on the board.

## Stop decision

The promised stop criterion was Setup=0 and Hold=0 with PSRAM DDR timing/attachment independently verified before SRAM-only programming. The new build still has Setup=1, slack -0.570 ns; therefore the candidate is **NOT authorized to program**.

Do not automatically reduce the PSRAM frequency, waive the cross-phase violation, patch more HDL, or use a Flash/BL702 programmer as an alleged timing fix. The next stage (PicoRV32 PSRAM adapter, CPU execution, FAT32 load) is **NOT STARTED** and depends on a separately qualified memory interface.

This closes the LAB-07 *attempt* as a reproducible pre-board timing failure, preserving source, both immutable SHA-256s and the UART unit-test improvement. It makes **no claim that the physical PSRAM is defective**.
