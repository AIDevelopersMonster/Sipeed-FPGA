## Important electrical discrepancy

The Gowin report for the original image had `clk_i` on pin52 in Bank1 with `IO_TYPE=LVCMOS33` but `BankVccio=1.2`. Onboard 27MHz oscillator is documented as 3.3V; Tang Nano 9K reference constraints commonly use `BANK_VCCIO=3.3` for this input. GPIO LEDs bank3 1.8V, reset bank3 1.8V, UART bank2 3.3V were otherwise consistent.

This may be the tool's unstated default bank voltage rather than the board's real measured supply; the report alone cannot establish the board's actual voltage. It is nevertheless a discrepancy in the programming candidate that should not be ignored. Therefore **DO NOT PROGRAM the original SHA** until explicitly corrected and the new fitted report is verified.

Project changed only its own `constraints/lab061_nano9k.cst` to add `BANK_VCCIO=3.3` for `clk_i`. Upstream, RV32I firmware and IMEM are unchanged. A new fit and new SHA are required. Updated `tools/audit_fit_lab061.ps1` checks fitted BankVccio for all ten ports; PASS cannot be asserted for original image. External UART RX is async; reset requires independent physical smoke.
