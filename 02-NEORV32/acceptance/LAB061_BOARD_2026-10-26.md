## Board LEDs: visually confirmed (three lit)

The firmware writes GPIO `0x15` at startup and `0x2A` after transmitting the banner; the RTL wrapper inverts LED pins. Therefore expected steady display is **three alternating LEDs ON**, bits 1,3,5 ON, bits 0,2,4 OFF. The operator supplied a photograph of the powered Tang Nano 9K and explicitly confirmed in the chat: **«Горят три светодиода»** (three LEDs are illuminated). This validates the three-LED *count* on physical hardware. Due to the blurred image and lack of six individual LED bit labels, the photograph does not independently verify that the illuminated LEDs correspond precisely to bits 1, 3 and 5; the exact `0x2A` mapping has not been electrically read back.
